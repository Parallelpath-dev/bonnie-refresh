Bonnie Plants Brand Refresh Proposal

What Bonnie asked for

A refresh that makes Bonnie the trusted live-goods lifestyle brand for every gardener.

A brand strategy and master brand refresh, a clear portfolio and sub-brand system, and guidelines your teams can put to work.

Success means the brand builds relevance, creates portfolio clarity and enables execution. It should strengthen trust, inspire casual gardeners, clarify the portfolio, take Bonnie beyond edibles, work across channels and earn adoption inside Bonnie.

$300k, in three phases

$100k each for Define + Discover, Develop the Brand and Enable + Activate. Each phase starts once the one before it is approved.

What Parallel Path proposes

A refresh built in three phases, with your team at every step.
